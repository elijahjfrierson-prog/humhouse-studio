# HumHouse Studio Privacy Policy

HumHouse Studio does not collect, sell or share personal data.

- **Your music stays yours.** Songs, recordings, samples and settings are saved on your device (and in folders you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive). We never upload them.
- **Microphone.** The app uses the microphone only to record audio you ask it to record. Recordings are stored on your device.
- **Purchases.** HumHouse Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.
- **No accounts, ads, tracking or analytics** are built into the iPhone app.
